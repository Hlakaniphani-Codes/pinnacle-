Service 01

Permanent Recruitment

We run the whole process: role briefing, advertising, headhunting, screening, competency interviews, references, offer management and onboarding. It covers roles from entry level to executive.

  • ID, qualification, criminal and credit checks as standard
  • Shortlist within 48 hours for most roles
  • Replacement guarantee, and success based fees
Service 02

Executive Search

When a role is confidential, scarce skilled or board level, we map the market, approach passive candidates directly and manage a structured assessment process with an Employment Equity slate.

  • Market map and target list agreed up front
  • Competency interviews and psychometric options
  • Retained model with staged milestones
Service 03

Contract & Temporary Staffing

For seasonal peaks, projects, cover and trial to hire. Workers are employed by Pinnacle. We handle contracts, payroll, statutory deductions, leave, PPE and replacements, on one weekly or monthly invoice.

  • On site supervisors for large teams
  • Compliant with LRA deeming provisions and BCEA
  • Same day replacement of absentees where possible
Service 04

Payroll & Workforce Outsourcing

Move a team, a department or a whole support function onto Pinnacle. We manage employment contracts, monthly payroll, payslips, EMP201/EMP501, UIF, SDL, COIDA and IRP5s, with a self service portal for staff.

  • Reduces fixed overhead and HR admin load
  • Monthly compliance and headcount reporting
  • POPIA compliant handling of all employee data
Service 05

Labour & IR Consulting

Our consultants support you with contracts and policies, disciplinary enquiries, retrenchment process, CCMA conciliation and arbitration, and Employment Equity plans and reporting.

  • Chairing of disciplinary hearings
  • CCMA representation and case preparation
  • EE committee facilitation and DoL submissions
Service 06

RPO & Bulk Recruitment

For new sites, growing contracts and seasonal hiring, we embed a dedicated recruitment team, using your brand and ATS, or ours, with assessment centres and weekly reporting against targets.

  • Assessment centres and mass screening days
  • Fixed monthly fee, predictable cost per hire
  • Community and local labour sourcing for site projects

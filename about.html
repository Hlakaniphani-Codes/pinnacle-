Recruitment built on accountability

Pinnacle exists on one conviction: employers deserve a staffing partner as diligent about compliance and delivery as it is about finding great people.

Our story

From specialist placements to full workforce partner

Pinnacle started placing finance and administrative professionals. As clients asked us to take on more, contract teams, payroll, labour advice, we built the systems and accreditations to do it properly.

Today we support employers in Gauteng, KwaZulu Natal, the Western Cape and the Eastern Cape across permanent recruitment, temporary staffing, executive search and outsourced workforce management.

We are a proudly South African, majority black owned business with a verified BBBEE Level 1 rating, so working with us strengthens your own procurement scorecard.

Our mission

Why we exist

To give every client a workforce they can rely on and be proud of, recruited fairly, managed compliantly and supported for the long term.

Our vision

Where we're going

To be the staffing partner South African employers name in a tender with confidence, recognised for governance, delivery and the calibre of people we place.

What we stand for

Five values we hire and fire on

  • 01
    Do what we said

    If we commit to a shortlist by Thursday, it lands by Thursday, or you hear from us before then.

  • 02
    Compliance is not optional

    Every placement is documented, every worker correctly employed, every statutory return filed.

  • 03
    Honest market advice

    We would rather tell you the salary is wrong than send candidates who will decline the offer.

  • 04
    People, not CVs

    We meet candidates, verify their claims and represent them accurately, to you and to themselves.

  • 05
    Long term over quick fee

    The replacement guarantee exists because we would rather recruit again than keep a fee we didn't earn.

How we work

A clear process, and a name against every step

  • 01
    Discovery

    We sit with your team, understand the role, the culture and the essentials, then commit to a delivery date in writing.

  • 02
    Search & screen

    Headhunting, advertising and our database, then screening against the brief and full compliance checks.

  • 03
    Shortlist

    A tight shortlist with consultant notes, salary expectations and availability, for most roles within 48 hours.

  • 04
    Offer & onboard

    We manage offers, references and start dates, then check in through the guarantee period.
